Settings

Billing

The values from the signed contract. The rate applies to billed time: each work item counts for its exact time, and only the total of the statement or invoice is rounded.

Contractor

On invoices and statements: name, legal form, address on two lines, SIREN, email and phone. The SIRET and the APE code stay saved but are not printed.

Bank details

Printed in the "Payment terms" of invoices: account holder, bank, IBAN in groups of four, BIC. Copy them from your bank details slip (RIB).

Client

Assignment areas

The areas of the contract. Each area has its own colour, used on cards, the log, the statement and invoices: click the dot to change it. An area used by log entries cannot be deleted.

Social contributions (estimate)

Used for cash flow and the URSSAF quarters in the Invoices view. Contributions are calculated on the revenue collected; the first declaration covers the quarter in which the activity started and the next one.

Reminders and notifications

Sablier watches deadlines, dated tasks, months to invoice, invoices awaiting payment, a forgotten timer and a neglected log. Everything shows up in the reminders center (sidebar) and on the dashboard; new items are also sent to the system notification center.

Data

The data is saved in readable files on this computer.

About

Sablier, assignment tracking for freelancers.

The monthly routine at a glance

1

Log as you go

Use the timer while you work, or add an entry in “Log and statement” (CtrlN).

2

Check the statement

At the end of the month, “Log and statement” shows the work day by day and calculates the billed time.

3

Create the invoice

One click creates the numbered invoice and saves the invoice and the statement as PDFs in the invoices folder (Settings).

4

Record the payment

On the “Invoices and income” page, mark the invoice as paid when the transfer arrives, and the income ledger fills itself in.

5

Declare to URSSAF

Each quarter, the income table gives the collected revenue to declare.

Page by page

Reminders
  • What Sablier watches: deadlines (each with its own reminder delay, otherwise the one from Settings), dated tasks, past months not yet invoiced, invoices whose payment is approaching or overdue, a timer that has been running for too long, and a log with no entry for several days.
  • Where they appear: at the top of the dashboard, in the reminders center (Reminders button in the sidebar, CtrlShiftR), on the badge of the Reminders button, and as a system notification. A reminder can be snoozed until tomorrow or for a week, or marked done.
  • The first time one is sent, your system may ask for permission to show Sablier's notifications: accept, or set it later in your system notification settings.
Log and timer

Log entries

  • Day by day: the “Log and statement” page shows one row per day (work items, billed time, amount). One click expands it into its entries, which you can edit, duplicate, delete or resume with the timer.
  • Add an entry (button at the top of the page, or CtrlN): date, area, work item (the label on the statement), optional details, then start and end or a duration (1h30, 45 min, 2.5).
  • Timer: the button at the bottom of the sidebar starts the counter; you name the work item when you stop it. It shows in a banner at the top of the screen, survives closing the application, and creates the entry when you stop it. Shortcut: CtrlShiftT, pause with CtrlAltT.
  • Resume: on an existing entry, this button restarts the timer with the same labels.
  • Linked task: in an entry's sheet, attach it to a task: its time is added to the task's time spent, and the entry shows the task name in the log (one click opens the task sheet). A timer started from a card makes this link by itself.
  • Billing month: in an entry's sheet, it defaults to the month of the work. Choose a later month to carry this work on a later statement and invoice: the entry keeps its date in the log and shows the moved to October badge. Useful for work done before the contract started.

Calculation rule

Entries of the same day that share the same area and the same work item label form a single work item. Each work item counts for its exact time; only the total of the statement or invoice is rounded up to the next quarter hour, once (article 6.1 of the contract; rule of 26 October 2026). Neither the split into entries nor the number of sessions changes what the client pays. A work item is always moved as a whole: its entries share the same billing month.
Log and statement, invoices
  • The statement follows the contract template: date, area, nature of the work, time spent, billed time, amount; on screen it reads day by day, and the PDF keeps the full detail. Authorized travel expenses are added on receipts. An entry can be non-billable (€0, reason shown) or carry a flat fee.
  • Work done: the tasks completed during the month can be attached to the statement as an appendix, to give the client a view of what moved forward (the first ones are shown, “See more” reveals the rest).
  • Create the invoice locks the month's entries that are not yet invoiced: continuous number (year-number), issue date, due date at thirty days, payment terms (late payment penalties and the €40 fixed recovery fee always printed) with your bank details. A month can carry several invoices. Only the next number is accepted, and the date cannot be earlier than that of the previous invoice. An issued invoice can no longer be edited; only the latest one can be deleted, in case of an error before sending, and never once it is paid. The month's invoice and statement are saved as PDFs in the invoices folder chosen in Settings, in the month's subfolder (“2026-11 November”), created if needed; “Regenerate the PDFs” adds a copy (“Invoice-2026-01 (2).pdf”) without touching the PDFs already saved: contractor, client and statement stay as they were at issue.
  • Move to another month moves work items to the statement and invoice of a later month, without touching their date: work done in September can thus be invoiced in October. The statement it leaves and the one it arrives on each announce it with a banner, and the entry carries the note “Done in September 2026” on screen as well as on the statement PDF. The invoice itself does not say when the work was done: no date and no original month, and its appendix groups work items by area and label, with times and amounts added up; the trace remains in the log and the statement. A work item already carried by an invoice can no longer be moved.
  • Invoices and income (tab under “Log and statement”) shows the issued invoices, the income ledger (filled in by marking each invoice as paid) and the table of URSSAF quarters, with estimated contributions (ACRE rate until 30 September 2027, full rate after that) and a “Declared” checkbox for each quarter.
Tasks, notes and deadlines
  • Tasks: three columns (To do, In progress, Done). Drag cards from one column to another; they sort themselves by priority, then by due date. One click opens the sheet: steps to tick off (progress shows on the card), attachments (files and images dragged from your file manager, links, code snippets), linked note pages, a dated progress log, time spent based on the log. From a card, Timer starts the counter on that task.
  • Ideas: they are written on the “Loose ideas” notes page (Idea style: light bulb button in the toolbar, / then “Idea”, or ? at the start of a line). Inside an idea, Enter starts a new line for the details; pressing Enter twice leaves it. The Turn into a task button creates the task in “To do”, linked to the page; the Task button in the toolbar does the same for any line.
  • Notes: pages and subpages, like a notebook. A page is written in one go, like a text document: Enter starts a new line, ShiftEnter goes to the next line without changing paragraph. The toolbar above the page sets the style of the selected lines (text or heading, lists, checkboxes, quote, callout, idea, divider), bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight and links. From the keyboard: / on an empty line to insert; # heading, - list, 1. numbered list, [] checkbox, > quote at the start of a line; CtrlShiftL checkbox, CtrlShift7 list, CtrlAlt1 to 3 headings, CtrlAlt0 text; Tab indents a list line, AltShift↑ or ↓ moves it; CtrlEnter ticks a checkbox; CtrlZ undoes. Pasted text always arrives without formatting. The search on the left also searches the content of pages. Under the title, the “Linked tasks” strip connects the page to tasks, in both directions. Everything is saved as you type.
  • Deadlines: the administrative calendar of the micro-business (ACRE, CFE, URSSAF declarations, vigilance certificate, summer 2027 review) and your own reminders, each with its own reminder delay.
Data and backups
  • Everything is saved in readable JSON files, in the application's data folder (button in Settings). A dated copy is made automatically once a day, even if the application stays open, and the last thirty are kept. Files attached to tasks are copied into the Pieces subfolder.
  • Export a backup… produces a single file to keep elsewhere (attached files are not in it: back up the whole data folder with your usual backup tool); Import a backup… restores it.
  • No personal data about your clients belongs here: the log describes your work, not your clients.
